# 🏈 NFL Offensive Performance & Betting Odds Analysis

An empirical analysis of 19,000+ player performances across 1,600+ NFL games using R to evaluate team resilience, position value, and betting market predictability.

---

## 💡 Executive Summary & Key Findings

* **Post-Blowout Resilience:** Teams losing by **10+** points ran more pass and rush plays in subsequent games, with passing yards showing higher variance but positive skewness.
* **Fantasy Position Variance:** Quarterbacks consistently yielded the highest average fantasy points with significant variance, while tight ends averaged the lowest due to blocking roles.
* **Home-Field Advantage Bias:** Underdogs played at home significantly less often than visiting underdogs, as home-field advantage inherently shifts close matchups in favor of the home team.
* **Betting Predictability:** Even the most predictable teams in the NFL (New Orleans Saints at 23%) exhibited massive performance variance relative to spread and over/under markets.
